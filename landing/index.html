How it plays

Three steps to your first win

  1. 1

    Create your hero

    Pick a species, a body form, a fighting style, colours and a name. One hero, yours for good.

  2. 2

    Gear up

    Eight slots: main weapon, off-hand, special, potion, helmet, chest, legs and boots. There are no classes. Your gear is your build.

  3. 3

    Watch the duel

    Hit Fight and the AI takes over. Speed it up, change the camera, or just enjoy the show.

Why it's different

Every fight tells its own story

Adaptive AI

An AI that reads the fight

Fighters look ahead before they move. They bait, dodge, parry, back off to catch a breath and punish openings, and you can read what they're thinking in little bubbles as it happens.

  • Changes tempo as the clock runs down
  • Plays around your items, not a fixed script
  • At 60 seconds night falls: overtime, double damage

Supers & ultimates

Supers that hit like a movie

Fill the energy bar and your hero cuts loose: widescreen bars, the camera leans in, time slows and the move's name slams onto the screen. Every main weapon has its own super, and skins play it in their own colours.

Collect

357 skins and 26 epic sets

Win gems, open chests and forge spares into rarer ones. Hunt for epic sets that reshape every piece and wrap your hero in a set aura, then pick a grand entrance to walk in with.

  • Rare, mythic, legendary and epic tiers
  • Daily shop and a featured set on sale
  • All pixel art, every frame drawn for the game

Compete

Arena Cup and online duels

Take on a 32-fighter knockout cup: five rounds, lose once and you're out. Bring up to 7 friends into the same bracket, or challenge a friend to a best-of-five online duel. Level up your hero and spend stat points as you go.

Arenas

Living arenas

Fight on Skygrove Isle, a floating island that runs from noon to golden hour to night during the round, or under the planets of the Astral Sanctum.

Get the game

Install it like an app

Arena Legends is a web app. Install it and it gets its own icon, opens full screen and keeps working offline. No app store, nothing heavy to download.

Computer

Windows, Mac, Linux · Chrome or Edge

  1. Tap Install on computer. The game opens.
  2. Click Install in the box that pops up.
  3. Launch it from the Start menu, Dock or desktop.
Install on computer

Android

Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet

  1. Tap Install on Android. The game opens.
  2. Tap Install, then confirm.
  3. Play from the icon on your home screen. Hold the phone sideways.
Install on Android

iPhone & iPad

Safari

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap Share at the bottom.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
